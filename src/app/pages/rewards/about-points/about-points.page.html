What is NETS Points?

NETS Points is our loyalty rewards program — turning your everyday spending, daily habits, and challenges into points you can redeem for real vouchers or send to friends.

How to Earn

Spend with NETS Earn 1 point for every $1 you spend at any merchant.
Daily & Weekly Quests Complete simple everyday tasks for bonus points.
Partner Challenges Visit or spend at partner merchants for bigger rewards.
Daily Check-In Open the app and check in each day — rewards grow the longer your streak.

What Points Can Do

Redeem Vouchers Trade points for real discounts at the Rewards Marketplace — they apply automatically the next time you pay that merchant.
Send to Friends Share points with anyone else on NETS, with an optional message attached.

Limits

A couple of fair-use limits keep the rewards program balanced for everyone — most people never come close to hitting either one.

300 pts / day Maximum you can earn per day, from any combination of spending, quests, and challenges.
20 / day Maximum number of transactions that can earn points each day.
5,000 pts Maximum points balance you can hold at once. Redeem or send some before you can earn more.
